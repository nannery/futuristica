About the Artist

I'm a photographer, illustrator and ceramicist from Metro Detroit. Downtown Detroit has been part of my life for a long time, and I've loved watching it come back to life.

Through my photographs, drawings and ceramics I want to share Detroit and its suburbs with everyone. I'm especially drawn to public transit: the People Mover, the QLine, trains, buses, and the subways I hope the city builds someday.

Take a look around my portfolio, and feel free to contact me with any questions.
